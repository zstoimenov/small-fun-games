⚙️ Settings

How long is your grid?

8 steps is one bar. 16 steps is two bars: longer tunes and fancier beats. Going to 16 copies your song into both halves.

How to play

  1. Tap a box on the grid to add a sound. Tap it again to take it away.
  2. Coloured rows are notes: high at the top, low at the bottom. Grey rows are drums.
  3. The big numbers along the top are the beats.
  4. ▶ Play loops your music. Change things while it plays!
  5. 🔊 Sound on! Some levels are all about listening.
