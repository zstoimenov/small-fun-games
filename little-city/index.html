How to play

  1. Pick a building from the tools, then tap a grass square. Roads: tap or drag.
  2. Every building must touch a road that leads OUT of town.
  3. 👆 Look: tap a house to hear what the family needs. 😀 happy, 😐 OK, 😟 unhappy.
  4. Be the Mayor: build, then press ▶ End year. Happy homes fill up, unhappy ones empty out, and taxes come in.
  5. Every family is different. The badge on 🏠 House shows who moves in next: 👵 grandparents like peace and quiet, 👨‍👩‍👧 young families want a school and park, 👷 workers want jobs, 🌿 nature lovers want trees or water next door.
  6. Look at the land. 🪨 Rocky ground doubles a factory's earnings, a road to the 🏰 castle brings tourists, and two shops side by side make a high street.
  7. Every 4 years there's an election. Rivals point at what's wrong: unhappy families, a loan, or coins nobody is spending. Fix it to win their votes!
