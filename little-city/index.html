Little City

Build a town where everyone is happy!

In grown-up words

You did it!

👷 Who plans and runs towns?

💬 Who decided where your school, park and shops would go? Ask a grown-up who your local councillor is!

How to play

  1. Pick a building from the bar under the map, then tap a grass square.
  2. Roads: tap or drag your finger. Every building must touch a road that leads OUT of town.
  3. 👆 Look: tap a house to hear what the family needs. 😀 happy, 😐 OK, 😟 unhappy.
  4. Yellow squares show how far a school, clinic or park reaches.
  5. 🧹 Bulldoze clears anything you built.
