How to play

  1. Pick a building from the tools, then tap a grass square. Roads: tap or drag.
  2. Every building must touch a road that leads OUT of town.
  3. 👆 Look: tap a house to hear what the family needs. 😀 happy, 😐 OK, 😟 unhappy.
  4. Be the Mayor: run your own town, year by year. Press ? on the Mayor's screen for the mayor's guide.

🎉 Your farewell party

🏛️ The mayor's guide

📅 Each year

  1. Build with the coins in the treasury: pick a tool, then tap the map.
  2. Press ▶ End year. Happy homes fill up, unhappy ones empty out, and taxes come in.
  3. Every building must touch a road that leads OUT of town.

👪 Families

  • Tap a house with 👆 Look to hear what the family needs. 😀 happy, 😐 OK, 😟 unhappy.
  • The badge on 🏠 House shows who moves in next: 👵 grandparents want a clinic and peace and quiet, 👨‍👩‍👧 young families want a school and park, 👷 workers want jobs, 🌿 nature lovers want trees or water next door.
  • As the town grows, everyone wants more: a shop, a school, a clinic and so on.

💰 Money

  • Everyone pays tax. Shops and factories earn from people close by. Schools and clinics cost coins every year.
  • Short of coins? Borrow at the 🏦 bank, but loans cost interest. Lots of coins? Spend them: tap a building to upgrade it.

🗺️ The land

  • 🪨 Rocky ground doubles a factory's earnings. A road to the 🏰 castle brings tourists. Two shops side by side make a high street.
  • Homes love a park or water next door, but floods can reach the waterside.

🚗 Traffic

  • Every 4 people make a car. Orange roads are busy; red roads are jammed, and families stuck in a jam aren't happy.
  • Tap a road to widen it, or build another way out so cars can go round.

🗳️ Elections and 🎯 missions

  • Every 4 years the town votes. Rivals point at what's wrong; fix it before the vote to win families back. The live poll shows how you're doing.
  • Each town has 3 missions. Tap 🎯 next to the coins to see them.

🎖️ Retiring

  • After 20 years you can retire whenever you like; after 10 terms (40 years) you must. Your farewell party spends the treasury on gifts for the town, then shows your legacy: Good, Great or Legendary mayor.

💡 Stuck? When you're not building, the panel next to the map gives you a tip.
