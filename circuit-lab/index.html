Lab Rules

Before you start, read these out loud. They keep you safe from real electricity.

    Toy batteries are safe to learn with. Wall power is not.
    🔥

    Short circuit!

    The electricity took a shortcut, so a huge amount rushed round and the battery got hot.

    In a real house, this is how fires start. That's why houses have safety switches that cut the power in a flash.

    Never try to fix a power point, a plug or a cord yourself. Tell a grown-up. Only a licensed electrician fixes wiring.

    👷 Who works with circuits?

    💬 Know a grown-up who does one of these jobs? Ask them what the best part is!

    How to play

    1. Pick a part from the box under the board.
    2. Tap a gap between two dots to put it there.
    3. Tap a switch to flip it on or off.
    4. 🧽 Remove takes a part back. Tapping a part with the same part picked takes it back too.
    5. Moving yellow dots show the electricity flowing.
    6. Stars: 3 if you use the fewest moves, fewer if you use lots.
    7. 🦺 Stay Safe is the most important chapter. Real wall power is never a toy.
