🚢 Battleship

Also called Морски бой. Hide your ships in the sea, then call out squares until you've found every one of theirs. Whoever sinks the other fleet first wins.

Another go after a hit

Hit something and you keep firing until you miss. It makes for a much quicker, much wilder game — and a lucky run can end it very fast.

Sit opposite

The screen turns around for the second player, so you can leave the device on the table between you instead of picking it up.

Help me

Puts a 💡 Hint button on the board that names a square and the reason for it, and says something useful after every shot.

What was Robo thinking?

It counts every way each ship it hasn't sunk could still be lying — every spot that doesn't run off the edge, cross a splash, or cross a wreck — and shoots wherever most of them cross. Bright squares are the likely ones.
