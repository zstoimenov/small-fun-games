How to play

  1. Every mission teaches new letters. Finish one to open the next.
  2. 👥 Together steps need a grown-up who speaks Bulgarian. They say the sounds and listen to you read.
  3. 🙋 On your own steps you can do by yourself. Match pictures to words and build words from letters.
  4. Stars come from the on-your-own steps. Get it right first time for 3 stars.
  5. History cards open as you learn. Read the Bulgarian together, then the English.
  6. 🛂 Passport: each mission opens a quiz about a place in Bulgaria. Get all 5 right for the stamp. No help used? It's gold!
  7. Tricky letters look like English letters but make a different sound. Practise them any time.

For grown-ups: the game has no sound on purpose. Computer voices say Bulgarian letters wrong. Sit with your child for the Learn, Blend and Read steps.
