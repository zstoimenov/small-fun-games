How to play

  1. Tap the tiles to build a sentence, one piece at a time.
  2. Tap a word in your sentence to pick it. New tiles go in front of it; tap it again to take it out.
  3. ✏️ My own words lets you type anything you like.
  4. 🔊 Read it reads your writing aloud. Listening is how real writers check their work!
  5. The story mountain has five parts: opening, build-up, problem, fix and ending.
