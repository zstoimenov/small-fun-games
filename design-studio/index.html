🧪 Mix a paint

How to play

  1. Mixing: tap a paint to add a drop. You can't take a drop out, so go slowly!
  2. Too much? Tap 🫗 Empty the pot and start again.
  3. Posters: tap anything to change its colour or size. Drag it to move it.
  4. Briefs: a customer tells you what they want. Press Check when it fits.
  5. Studio: make any poster you like, with paints you mixed yourself.
