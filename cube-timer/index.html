How it works

  1. Mix the cube up using the moves at the top. The picture shows what it should look like when you've done it right.
  2. Hold the big button. It turns orange, then green when it's ready.
  3. Let go and solve. The clock starts the moment your fingers leave the screen.
  4. Tap anywhere to stop. Your time gets saved, and a new mix-up is ready for the next go.

If a corner was left a little bit turned, tap +2. If you gave up, tap Didn't solve. Tapped it by accident? 🗑 throws that one away.
