Meet Isaac Newton

Newton lived in England more than 300 years ago. The story goes that he saw an apple fall from a tree and started to wonder why things move.

He worked out three rules that everything follows. Play with each one and see it for yourself!

In grown-up words

You did it!

How to play

  1. Pick a law. Each one is a rule about how things move.
  2. Try it first. The play area has no rules. Just have fun and watch.
  3. Then beat the levels. Get the thing to stop on the green flag.
  4. Stars: 3 if you get it first go, 2 in a few goes, 1 if it takes longer.
  5. Arrows show pushes. A bigger arrow means a bigger push.
  6. Finish all three laws, then try the quiz!
