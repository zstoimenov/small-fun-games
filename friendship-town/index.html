Friendship Town

You're the new kid in town. Make some friends!

In grown-up words

For grown-ups

Friendship Town retells the 30 ideas in Dale Carnegie's book How to Win Friends and Influence People (1936) as everyday kid moments. The words, people and stories are our own.

The book's own rule runs through every lesson: mean it. Fake praise and tricks never score in this game.

A good way to use it: play a lesson together, then ask "When could you try that this week?"

You did it!

How to play

  • 🏠 You just moved to Friendship Town. Visit the 4 places, one at a time.
  • 💬 Something happens. Pick what you would say or do.
  • ❤️ Kind and honest choices fill the friendship hearts.
  • ⭐ Fill all the hearts for 3 stars. Every lesson gives you a sticker for your Friend Book.
  • 🎮 Some lessons are little games: smile at neighbours, learn names, be a great listener.
