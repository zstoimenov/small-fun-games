👷 Who makes comics?

💬 What's your favourite comic, book or cartoon? Somebody wrote it, drew it and planned every page!

How to play

  1. Tap a panel in the strip to work on it.
  2. Add a scene, characters, words and props with the buttons.
  3. Tap a character to change how they feel. Drag anything to move it.
  4. Words: 💬 speech is said out loud, 💭 thoughts are secret, 📜 captions tell the story, 💥 sound words are loud!
  5. Briefs: an editor tells you what the comic needs. Press Check when it's ready.
