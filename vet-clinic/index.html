How to play

  1. Read what the owner says. It's your first clue.
  2. Pick your checks: heartbeat, temperature, eyes, mouth, skin, weight or X-ray.
  3. Green band = normal for that animal. The pin shows your patient.
  4. Only do the checks that fit. A check you didn't need costs a star.
  5. Then decide: what's wrong, how to treat it, and how to care for it at home.
  6. Wild animals: never touch one yourself. Tell a grown-up to call wildlife rescue.
