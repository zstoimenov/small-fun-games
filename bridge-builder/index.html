How to play

  1. Pick a material from the box: road, wood or steel.
  2. Drag from dot to dot to build. Keep dragging to build more. Or tap a dot, then a dot next to it.
  3. Road is what the truck drives on. It only goes flat, along the road line.
  4. Triangles on the riverbank pins ▲ hold the bridge up.
  5. Oops? Press ↶ Undo. Or pick 🧽 Remove and tap a piece to take it back.
  6. Test it! Green means easy, yellow means working hard, red means about to break.
  7. Stars: 3 if you use the fewest pieces.
  8. On a computer: keys 1-5 pick a part, R picks Remove, Ctrl+Z undoes, Space tests, Esc lets go of a dot.
  9. 🗺️ Bridge Trail: harder puzzles. Each world has a new rule: coins, crumbly cliffs, boats, snow and ropes.
