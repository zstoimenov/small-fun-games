Bridge Builder

🌉

Be a bridge engineer

A truck needs to cross the river. Build a bridge out of road, wood and steel, then press Test and watch it drive over.

Will it hold, bend, or snap? Colours show how hard each piece is working.

👷 Who builds bridges?

💬 Know a grown-up who does one of these jobs? Ask them what the best part is!

In grown-up words

You did it!

How to play

  1. Pick a material from the box: road, wood or steel.
  2. Tap a dot, then a dot next to it, to join them. Keep tapping to carry on from there.
  3. Road is what the truck drives on. It only goes flat, along the road line.
  4. Triangles on the riverbank pins ▲ hold the bridge up.
  5. Tap the middle of a piece to take it back.
  6. Test it! Green means easy, yellow means working hard, red means about to break.
  7. Stars: 3 if you use the fewest pieces.
